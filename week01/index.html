Haiti

Haiti is home to the magnificent Citadelle Laferrière, a historic fortress in northern Haiti that welcomes visitors from around the world. Its remarkable scenery has also reached international audiences through music. In 2018, the monument appeared in Jason Derulo's music video "Colors," the Coca-Cola anthem for the 2018 FIFA World Cup in Russia. The video also featured a special appearance by Wyclef Jean. The Citadelle stands as a powerful symbol of Haiti's history, independence, architecture, and cultural heritage.
